
# My CompTIA Security+ Journey
This is where I'm documenting my journey toward earning the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) certification and building my foundation in cybersecurity.

# Why I Started

I decided to start this journey because cybersecurity is something I've been interested in since I was laid off from my Business Analyst role in 2021.

At the time, I knew I was interested in cybersecurity, but I didn't really know which direction I wanted to take. There was also a lot changing in technology, especially with AI becoming more widely used.

Now, I feel like I have a better idea of where I want to go and the path I want to take.

Security+ is just the foundation for that path.

# Where I Want to Go

I see myself getting into GRC. I like making improvements to systems and policies, following rules, and solving problems for others.

GRC also feels similar to work I've done in other industries—finding problems, understanding what needs to improve, and then finding solutions.

I see this journey taking me from my first GRC role into areas like cloud and AI. Eventually, I want to teach what I've learned and start my own consulting company helping organizations improve their security, risk, and compliance programs.

# What Security+ Has Been Like

Security+ has been a journey for me. Honestly, it's been about three years in the making. I kept putting it off, and when I first started, it wasn't going well.

I like my current job at Johns Hopkins and think it's a great organization, but I also know that I want more for myself. For me, Security+ represents the beginning of that next step.

Studying doesn't feel as difficult to me anymore. I've found a routine that works for me, and I've seen a lot of improvement. Now I find myself wanting to learn more and keep getting better.

I wake up excited about it. I'll practice on my lunch break, on weekends, late at night, or early in the morning. It really doesn't matter to me anymore. My goal is to become job-ready and continue building from there.

One thing I've also realized is that some of these concepts aren't completely new to me. I've come across many of them throughout previous roles, including my time as a Business Analyst and in other analyst and representative positions going back to when I was 21.

Security+ has helped me reconnect with some of that past experience, refresh my knowledge, and start putting everything together from a cybersecurity perspective.
